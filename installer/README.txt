OpenFairway - offline play and mods for PGA TOUR 2K25
=====================================================

Unofficial community project. Not affiliated with 2K, HB Studios or the PGA TOUR.

Install
  1. Double-click "Install OpenFairway.cmd". It finds the game through Steam.
  2. Open "OpenFairway" from your desktop.
  3. Switch mods on or off and press Play.

Play starts the game through Steam with mods for that one launch, always offline.
Play without mods, or starting the game from Steam as usual, gives the normal online game.
"Uninstall OpenFairway.cmd" removes everything the installer added.

Mods live in <game>\OpenFairway\mods\. The log is <game>\OpenFairway\logs\openfairway.log.

Writing mods:  https://xk0fe.github.io/pga2k25-openfairway/
