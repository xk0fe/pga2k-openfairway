OpenFairway - offline play and mods for PGA TOUR 2K25
=====================================================

Unofficial community project. Not affiliated with 2K, HB Studios or the PGA TOUR.

Install
  1. Double-click "Install OpenFairway.cmd". It finds the game through Steam.
  2. Open "OpenFairway" from your desktop.
  3. Press Play. Mods, Settings and What's new are on the right.

Play starts the game through Steam with mods for that one launch, always offline, and the launcher confirms that
OpenFairway loaded. Play without mods, or starting the game from Steam as usual, gives the normal online game.
"Uninstall OpenFairway.cmd" removes everything the installer added.

Updates: when a new version is out, Play becomes Update; your mods, settings and saves stay.
New mods: drop a mod's .zip on the launcher window. In the game, F8 opens the mod manager.

Mods live in <game>\OpenFairway\mods\. Logs are in <game>\OpenFairway\logs\.
The launcher contacts GitHub only, to look for updates and read release notes; Settings can switch it off.
